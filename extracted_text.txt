AI-Driven Clinical Decision Support Framework for Personalized Hereditary Cancer Risk AssessmentProblem StatementHereditary cancers are caused by inherited genetic mutations that significantly increase an individual's risk of developing cancer. While genetic testing can identify these mutations, there is a need for a system that can predict an individual's lifetime cancer risk, estimate the likely age of disease onset, assess family risk, and provide personalized clinical recommendations. This project aims to develop an intelligent decision support system by combining genomic mutation data, clinical information, rule-based assessment, and machine learning techniques to support early detection and preventive healthcare.Project OverviewThe objective of this project is to develop an intelligent clinical decision support system for hereditary cancer risk assessment using genomic mutation data and clinical information. The system combines rule-based decision making with machine learning models to estimate an individual's lifetime cancer risk, predict the likely age of disease onset, assess family risk, and generate personalized clinical recommendations.Mutation datasets were obtained from cBioPortal, a publicly available cancer genomics database, while clinical information was obtained from the corresponding clinical datasets.Datasets UsedMutation datasets from the following cancer types were used:Breast Invasive Carcinoma (BRCA)Ovarian Serous Cystadenocarcinoma (OV)Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma (PAAD)Prostate Adenocarcinoma (PRAD)Uterine Corpus Endometrial Carcinoma (UCEC)Stomach Adenocarcinoma (STAD)Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (KIRC)Brain Lower Grade Glioma (LGG)Each mutation dataset contains information such as:Patient IDSample IDGene NameMutation TypeVariant ClassificationProtein ChangeThe clinical dataset contains patient-related information including:AgeGenderCancer StageSurvival StatusOverall Survival TimeTumor SiteTreatment DetailsRaceSmoking History (where available)The mutation and clinical datasets are merged using patient identifiers to create a comprehensive dataset for analysis and prediction.Important Genes UsedAlthough thousands of genes can mutate in cancer, certain genes are strongly associated with specific hereditary cancers. counselling recommendationFamily screening recommendationsPersonalized follow-up planOverall WorkflowPatient provides clinical history or uploads a genetic test report.The system checks whether hereditary genetic testing is required.If recommended, genetic testing identifies pathogenic variants.The pathogenic variant is used as input to the first machine learning model.ML Model 1 predicts the patient's lifetime hereditary cancer risk.The predicted risk is categorized into Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.Based on the risk category, the system performs family risk assessment using clinical guidelines.ML Model 2 predicts the expected age of disease onset.Finally, all outputs are combined to generate personalized screening, counselling, and follow-up recommendations.Model
